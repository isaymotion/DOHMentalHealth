# Release 19 · Pass 2 — Deep Study Guide layer

## Purpose

Pass 2 moves the Study Guide further from overview material toward a genuine revision resource. It keeps the existing 10-topic structure and adds a second depth layer to every topic.

## Added to every topic

- **Source-derived facts to master** — concise facts extracted from the supplied DOH/PCMH *National Mental Health Strategic Plan 2019–2023* PDF.
- **Self-test prompts** — open-recall questions designed to make the learner retrieve the source structure rather than only reread summaries.
- Existing learning objectives, detailed study notes, key distinctions, exam focus, and source locators are preserved.

## Source boundary

This pass uses only the supplied Strategic Plan PDF. It does not import current statistics, later policy developments, external legal commentary, or other sources. Historical targets remain historical targets. Proposed budgets remain planning figures. Legal and scanned-annex material is not reconstructed where the PDF does not clearly establish the detail.

## Important source-structure clarification

The PDF contains multiple levels of results language: the Theory of Change says the four pillars are divided into five outcomes; the main Outcomes section visibly enumerates four outcomes; and Annex 5 later contains seven numbered outcome headings. Pass 2 does not collapse these into a single invented list. Learners are prompted to recognize the document's own layers and read each section in context.
