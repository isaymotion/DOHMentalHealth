# MindPlan Release 20 — Pass 1
## Mental Health Act IRR study section

**Version:** v2.40.0 · October 2026

### Why this was added
The supplied National Mental Health Strategic Plan identifies Annex 2 as the Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR) of RA 11036 and places it at PDF pp. 74–92. Those pages are scanned legal text, so the existing Study Guide could identify the annex but could not safely provide a rule-by-rule transcription.

A web research pass located a 17-page online copy of the IRR. The copy is hosted by Scribd and is not treated as an official government repository. A DOH Memorandum Circular, No. 2019-0095 dated 4 February 2019, independently confirms that the approved IRR, signed on 22 January 2019, was attached for information and guidance.

### What the new topic contains
- dedicated Study Guide topic 11
- section map for IRR Sections 1–49
- chapter-level summaries of implementation requirements
- high-yield safeguards around informed consent, capacity, emergencies, restraint/confinement, advance directives, legal representatives and supported decision making
- internal review board functions
- community mental-health services and reporting
- suicide prevention and public awareness
- education/workplace provisions
- capacity building and research
- government-agency and LGU responsibilities
- PCMH and DOH Mental Health Division provisions
- penalties, appropriations and effectivity
- explicit source-trail and legal-use warning

### Source boundary
Plan-specific claims continue to use the supplied Strategic Plan. Rule-level content in this new topic is an explicitly disclosed external research expansion because the scanned Annex 2 did not support a trustworthy searchable transcription.

This is a study aid, not legal advice and not a substitute for the official promulgated IRR.
