- Front matter uses Roman numerals and should be cited by its Roman page label when relevant.

Do not apply the main-text +14 rule to annexes. For annex references, cite the PDF page and verify the printed label within the actual annex page.

## Recommended Study Guide chapter structure

1. **Start here: What this Plan is and is not** — scope, period (2019–2023), intended users, and historical status.
2. **Why the Plan was developed** — situation overview and documented gaps.
3. **Policy foundations** — the policy/legal context as presented in the Plan; links to Annexes 1–2.
4. **How the Plan was developed** — consultations, stakeholders, and planning process.
5. **How to read the strategic framework** — structure, theory of change, and hierarchy of results.
6. **Vision, mission, and three goals** — official wording plus plain-language learning paraphrase.
7. **The four pillars** — overview and relationships among Promotion and Prevention; Leadership and Governance; Services; Information and Research.
8. **Pillar chapter: Promotion and Prevention** — outcome(s), outputs, activities, indicators, and targets.
9. **Pillar chapter: Leadership and Governance** — outcome(s), outputs, activities, indicators, and targets.
10. **Pillar chapter: Services** — outcome(s), outputs, activities, indicators, and targets.
11. **Pillar chapter: Information and Research** — outcome(s), outputs, activities, indicators, and targets.
12. **Monitoring and evaluation** — indicator definitions, baselines, target years, data sources, formulas, reporting frequency, assumptions, and risks where stated.
13. **Budget and scorecards** — use annexes as reference material, with annex-specific pagination checks.
14. **Acronym and definitions reference** — searchable, source-faithful glossary.

This learning order reorganizes material for study while preserving the source's own headings and hierarchy as explicit references. It must not be presented as the Plan's official chapter order.

## Editorial rules for Pass B

- Every substantive paraphrase must carry a printed page and PDF page reference.
- Preserve names, numbers, dates, indicator wording, agencies, and technical terms unless a clearly labeled explanation follows.
- Keep source content separate from “Learning explanation” and “Application / interpretation.”
- Label 2020–2023 targets as **historical planned targets**, never as achieved results or current performance.
- If a detail is not in the source, say “The Plan does not specify this here” rather than filling the gap from general knowledge.
- Annexes 1 and 2 are legal materials appended to the Plan; identify them as annexed legal source material, not as newly authored Plan provisions.
- This outline does not certify every indicator/target value; detailed row-level verification is required before the target tables are paraphrased.
