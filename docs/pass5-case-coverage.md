# Pass 5 — Ten-case curriculum coverage

## Coverage review

The eight-case library already contained direct matches for The Missed Follow-up, The School Campaign, and The Local Mental Health Action Plan. Four other themes were represented under different titles: A Rights Concern (previously Rights at the Service Desk), The Fragmented Local System (previously The Local Governance Roundtable), The Disaster-Affected Community (previously After the Typhoon), and The Missing Data (previously Data Without Denominators).

Three requested cases were not yet implemented as distinct case experiences and have been added in Pass 5: The Barangay Referral Gap, The Person Behind the Policy, and The Funding Proposal. ## Safeguards

All scenarios are fictional teaching scenarios, not cases reported in the source. Source anchors use page mappings checked in Pass 4. Case-level actions, indicators, and co-design suggestions are learning interpretations or proposed indicators, not quoted Plan requirements. Local protocols and current resources must be verified separately. No specific diagnosis, clinical pathway, legal finding, funding source, or mandatory budget allocation is invented.
