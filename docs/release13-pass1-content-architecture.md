## 1. Editorial contract

1. Treat the supplied Plan as the primary and controlling source for the summaries. Do not silently add outside facts or update its historical descriptions.
2. Preserve source terminology, names, agency responsibilities, dates, quantities, definitions, indicator wording, baselines, targets, data sources, formulas, reporting frequencies, assumptions, and risks wherever present.
3. Distinguish **Source summary** from **Learning explanation** and any **Application / interpretation**. Interpretive text must not be mistaken for wording or conclusions in the Plan.
4. Label 2019–2023 goals, target values, milestones, and scorecard expectations as historical plan-period material. Do not describe them as achieved, current, or independently evaluated unless a source explicitly supports that claim.
5. Distinguish the Plan's narrative from legal materials appended as Annexes 1–2 and from tabular monitoring/scorecard artifacts in Annexes 3–5.
6. Preserve the document's own organization and also offer a learner-oriented navigation order. Clearly label the latter as MindPlan's study sequence, not the Plan's official chapter order.
7. Every substantive content block must include printed page label(s) where legible and the exact PDF page number(s). For annexes, verify labels directly on each page; do not apply the main-text +14 rule.
8. When extraction is incomplete, a table is ambiguous, or a figure contains information not represented in text, mark it for visual/source-page review rather than guessing.
9. The Plan's cited figures and historical statements should retain their stated date and attribution. Do not present them as current estimates.
10. Do not infer that a listed activity caused an outcome, that a target was met, or that an indicator is valid/reliable beyond what the source says.

## 2. Source pagination rules

- PDF length: 126 pages.
- Front matter includes Roman-numbered sections and blank/verso pages; PDF page numbers are the stable locator for the supplied file.
- Main narrative: printed Arabic page + 14 generally maps to the PDF page (e.g., printed p. 23 → PDF p. 37; printed p. 38 → PDF p. 52).
- Annexes are an exception. The combined PDF's annex-internal labels do not consistently match the table-of-contents page references. Use both the annex-internal printed label (when visible) and PDF page number; record discrepancies, never normalize them away.
- Annex 4 begins at PDF p. 113 with internal label 99; Annex 5 begins at PDF p. 115 with internal label 100. The table of contents instead lists them as pp. 118 and 120. Annex 3 starts at PDF p. 93 (internal label 79) and its scorecard tables continue through PDF p. 112.
- References occupy PDF pp. 125–126; contents page lists p. 123. Use the PDF locator and report the contents-page mismatch as a pagination note if needed.

## 4. Learner-facing chapter sequence (MindPlan navigation; not the official source order)

1. **Start here: scope and how to use the Plan** — Executive Summary, planning period, intended users, and historical-status note.
2. **Situation and rationale** — Overview of the Situation plus the documented gaps.
3. **Policy foundations** — Policies on Mental Health, with links to the Act and IRR annexes.
4. **How the Plan was developed** — Road to the Plan and participating sectors.
5. **How to read the framework** — Structure, Theory of Change, hierarchy of results, and the five outcomes/four pillars relationship.
6. **Vision, mission, and goals** — exact source wording, explanation, and recall aids.
7. **The four pillars at a glance** — pillar descriptions and outcome mapping.
8. **Promotion and Prevention** — comprehensive outcomes, outputs, activities, indicators, target years, agencies.
9. **Leadership and Governance** — same level of detail.
10. **Services** — same level of detail.
11. **Information and Research** — same level of detail.
12. **Monitoring and evaluation** — row-level Annex 3 guide and field glossary.
13. **Budget summary** — Annex 4 with pagination note and amount verification.
14. **Performance scorecards** — Annex 5's design, status legend and indicator reference.
15. **Mental Health Act reference** — Annex 1 index and section-level summaries.
16. **IRR reference** — Annex 2 index and rule-level summaries.
17. **Glossary** — source-defined acronyms and definitions.
18. **References** — source bibliography and traceability notes.

Each chapter should link back to the original source headings/pages so that the learner-oriented order never obscures where material came from.

## 5. Standard template for each comprehensive chapter

- Chapter title and source heading(s)
- Learning objectives (clearly authored as MindPlan learning aids)
- Core message / executive synthesis
- Structured comprehensive summary following source subheadings
- Key terms, dates, agencies, figures and named frameworks
- Tables/indicators: row-level structured representation where needed
- Source-defined limitations, assumptions and risks
- “Learning explanation” (explicitly distinguished from source content)
- High-yield recall cues (do not replace source details)
- 5–10 active-recall questions with answer feedback, proportionate to chapter length
- Source references: printed label + exact PDF page(s), and links to the corresponding source pages
- QA status: text checked / table checked / page image checked / cross-reference checked

For very large annex tables, retain a full structured table or field-by-field reference view in the app/data files and provide a narrative summary separately. Do not compress away targets, indicators, responsible agencies, or caveats merely to fit a short screen.
